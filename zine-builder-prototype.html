About this builder

What you can do

  • Add text scraps, a photo, a headline bar, a burst, a speech bubble, a circle badge, an arrow banner, a ticket stub, a stamp, an outlined note or a lightning bolt.
  • Add a gig listing pulled straight from the Socks On Records site (upcoming gigs only) — tap it in "+ Gig listing" and it drops in as editable text.
  • Pages, issues, publishing and the PDF export live under their own "📑 Pages" button, separate from the Menu.
  • Drag a piece to move it, drag its red dot to resize, drag its green dot to rotate — or use "Tilt it" / "Straighten" for a quick angle.
  • Tap a selected text piece again to edit the words.
  • Change a piece's text size (S–XXL), font and colour (invert), and replace a photo any time.
  • Turn the photocopier harshness up or down for the whole page, or keep one piece "clean" while the rest gets copied.
  • Reorder layers front-to-back, delete a piece, and pick the paper.
  • Add pages, switch between them, reorder them, and give each one a title. Group pages into issues, like folders.
  • Edit two facing pages as a spread (the "⇄" button next to a page in the Pages list) so an image or headline can run across the gutter — drag a piece past its own edge and it'll spill onto the facing page too.
  • Everyone signed in can see every page.
  • Everything saves automatically as you work — close the tab and come back any time.
  • Save the page as an image to look at or share outside the browser.
  • Export a whole issue as a PDF in plain page order, for reviewing — or as a booklet-order PDF, with pages paired and reordered onto sheet sides ready to send to a printer.
  • Mark an issue "Published" to make it visible in the digital flipbook reader on the website.

What it can't do (yet)

  • No undo — if you delete something or mess up a piece, you'll need to re-add it.
  • You can only edit or delete your own pages — someone else's page is read-only to you.
  • Spread editing shows the facing page as a snapshot, not a second live canvas — tap Swap to edit the other side.
  • No multi-select — pieces are moved and resized one at a time.
  • Undo is single-level — it only takes back the last thing you did.
  • The saved image is a best-effort snapshot — the photocopy filter sometimes doesn't carry over perfectly.
