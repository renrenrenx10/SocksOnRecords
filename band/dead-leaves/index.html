Dead Leaves

Peterborough, UK

Dead Leaves are a Peterborough (UK) based band, sharing a love for soaring melodies with fuzz gazer interludes. The name reflects a desire for a degree of humility mixed with an urge to create songs that matter and have meaning. A reaction to the what-you-see-is-what-you-get punk resurgence in recent years, Dead Leaves offer depth and soulfulness.
