Rats! Rats! Rats!

Gosport,UK

Rats! Rats! Rats! are a punk grunge two piece from Gosport, UK.

Formed in 2022, Andy Hawxwell (guitar/vocals) and Rich Oakes (drums/backing vocals) play fast, loud and tight. They have released 2 full albums on Horn and Hoof Records/Rare Vitamin Records ("Bloody Hello" in 2025 and "Heavy Petting at the Lido" in 2026)

They have completed 4 headline tours of England as well as support slots with punk legends Snuff, Ed Tudor Pole, Peter & the Test Tube Babies and ex-Sex Pistol Glen Matlock.

Andy and Rich both own rescue dogs. #projectgalgo
